# Castle character art

Generated using the built-in image generation tool with transparent output. Final game assets live in `assets/characters/`.

All four characters are cosmetic choices; they share the same scoring and abilities. Player or team colors remain visible around the character on the race map.

## Prompt set

Shared specification: transparent full-body character sprite for an Arabic castle-themed family quiz racing game. Polished hand-painted 2D cartoon fantasy art, warm olive skin, dark expressive eyes, friendly expression, readable silhouette and warm gold highlights. One character centered in a neutral front three-quarter standing pose, both feet visible, clear margins, no text, logos, floor or scene. Respectful Arab-inspired medieval fantasy with no modern national symbols.

- `guardian.png`: young adult Arab castle guardian, short dark beard, navy quilted tunic, brass shoulder armor, cream wrapped headcloth, small round shield, sheathed curved sword, confident smile.
- `archer.png`: young adult Arab woman archer, emerald long tunic with gold trim, cream headscarf, brown belt and boots, wooden bow at her side and small quiver on her back.
- `scholar.png`: elderly Arab castle scholar, neatly trimmed grey beard, burgundy robe with geometric gold embroidery, wrapped cream and burgundy turban, holding a closed blue book. Kindly expression; no magic effects.
- `scout.png`: young adult Arab woman scout, terracotta hooded cape, cream long tunic, brown belt and boots, small brass lantern at her side, welcoming smile, no weapon.
